About
The Balm Ethos

The Balm Ethos is a sustainability publication about the often unseen environmental journey of an everyday lip balm.

The idea is simple: make the story behind a familiar object easier to see. A small lip balm can involve raw materials, packaging decisions, transportation, customer habits and an end-of-life pathway.

Our approach

Follow the whole journey.

This website follows an eco-friendly lip balm through its full lifecycle. Each stage looks at the materials, decisions, movement and habits that shape its environmental story.

About the creator

A student perspective.

Hi, I’m Gowri P Midhun, the creator of The Balm Ethos.

I’m an MYP 5 student of the Choice School IB in Tripunithura, Kochi, Kerala. I decided to create this blog, The Balm Ethos, as part of my Design Eportfolio. However, what truly inspired me to choose this topic is because sustainability is such an intriguing and crucial topic in today’s era. As a part of this project, we had to explore the lifecycle and unseen design process behind any product’s creation.

We tend to overlook that our everyday actions can have a huge impact on our surroundings. Small choices can make a difference. Through this blog, I aim to educate all readers, specifically environmental enthusiasts, that something as simple as a lip balm can make a difference if chosen properly.

The takeaway

Notice the choices.

The goal is not perfection. It is to notice the choices inside everyday objects and to make more thoughtful decisions wherever possible.
